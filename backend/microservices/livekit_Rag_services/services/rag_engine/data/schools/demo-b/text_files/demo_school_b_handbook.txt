Demo School B - Parent Handbook 2026-27

This handbook is for a made-up demonstration school, used to show that one Vocira voice assistant can serve more than one school. Every detail below belongs to Demo School B only.

About Demo School B
Demo School B is a co-education school in Karachi for Playgroup to Grade 10. It follows the Sindh Board curriculum up to Grade 8 and the Matric (SSC) programme in Grades 9 and 10. The school was founded in 2011 and is run by the Demo Education Trust. The principal is Mrs. Sana Qureshi.

Campus and Contact
Head office and main campus: Plot 12, Block 5, Clifton, Karachi.
Helpline: 021-111-000-222 (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
Email: info@demoschoolb.example
Parents can also visit the front office in person during office hours.

School Timings
Playgroup to Grade 2: 7:45 AM to 12:30 PM.
Grades 3 to 10: 7:45 AM to 1:30 PM.
Friday: all classes end at 12:00 PM.
In winter (December and January) school starts at 8:15 AM.

Admission Procedure
1. Collect the admission form from the front office or download it from the school website.
2. Submit the form with a copy of the child's B-Form, two photographs and the last school's result card.
3. The child sits an entry test in English, Urdu and Mathematics (no test for Playgroup and Nursery).
4. Parents attend a short meeting with the Head of Section.
5. On selection, pay the admission fee within 7 days to confirm the seat.
Admissions for the new session open on 1 February and close on 15 March.

Fee Structure 2026-27 (monthly)
Playgroup and Nursery: Rs. 8,000 per month.
Grades 1 to 5: Rs. 9,500 per month.
Grades 6 to 8: Rs. 11,000 per month.
Grades 9 and 10: Rs. 12,500 per month.
Admission fee (one time): Rs. 25,000.
Annual charges: Rs. 6,000, paid in August.
Siblings: the second child gets 10 percent off the monthly fee, the third child 15 percent.
Fees are paid by the 10th of every month at any Meezan Bank branch or through the bank app. A late fine of Rs. 500 applies after the 10th.

Uniform
Boys: light blue shirt with the school monogram, navy blue trousers, maroon blazer in winter, black shoes.
Girls: light blue shirt with the monogram, navy blue shalwar or skirt, white dupatta from Grade 6, maroon blazer in winter, black shoes.
Uniforms are sold only at the school uniform shop on campus.

Academic Year and Holidays
The academic year runs from August to June.
First term: August to December. Second term: January to June.
Summer vacation: 1 June to 31 July. Winter break: 22 December to 1 January.
Mid-term exams are in October and March; final exams are in May.
Result cards are given at the Parent-Teacher Meeting two weeks after each exam.

Facilities
Science and computer laboratories, a library, an indoor sports hall, a swimming pool for Grades 4 and above, and an art room.
School transport is available on selected routes in Clifton, DHA and Saddar for Rs. 4,500 per month.

Rules for Parents
Attendance below 85 percent in a term needs a written reason from the parent.
For a leave, send an application to the class teacher the same day.
Mobile phones are not allowed for students on campus.

About Vocira at Demo School B
Vocira is the school's AI voice assistant. It answers general questions about Demo School B - admissions, fees, timings, uniform, holidays and facilities - in English and Urdu. Children's personal records (attendance, fees paid, results) are not available on Vocira for Demo School B yet; for those, parents should call the school helpline.
