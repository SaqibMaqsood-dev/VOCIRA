ABOUT THE EDUCATORS:
====================
The Educators is a private school network owned and operated by Beaconhouse.
It was launched in 2002 with the aim to provide standardized quality education across Pakistan at an affordable fee.
Today it has 1,000+ campuses in 275+ cities with over 240,000 students enrolled.
The Educators is the largest private school network in Pakistan.
Medium of instruction is English.
Curriculum follows international standards.

ADMISSION PROCEDURE:
====================
Step 1: Schedule for date of interview and admission test is announced by the school.
Step 2: An oral and written test is conducted as per schedule.
Step 3: A child must first clear the written test before qualifying for interview. Written test is not applicable for pre-school.
Step 4: The child and parents are interviewed by the School Head and possibly attending staff members.
Step 5: If the child clears the admission test and interview, the admission fee invoice is submitted at school to complete the process.

Note: Deposit fee at the designated bank or school along with the complete registration form.
Online Registration available at: www.educators.edu.pk/online-registration.php

ADMISSION TEST DETAILS:
=======================
Pre-School (PG, Nursery, KG): No written test. Oral test only.
KG and Class I: English, Urdu, Mathematics and General Knowledge (oral).
Class II to X(class 2nd to 10th): English, Urdu, Mathematics and Science (written).
Each paper is for 100 marks spread evenly across all subjects.
Test fits onto one page.
No other personnel other than designated invigilators are allowed during test.

ADMISSION POLICY:
=================
There is no written test for pre-school admission.
Admission for primary and secondary is based on written test, interview, and age criteria.
Students from any school background are welcome to apply.
Apply online at: www.educators.edu.pk/online-registration.php
Or visit your nearest campus directly.

==================
